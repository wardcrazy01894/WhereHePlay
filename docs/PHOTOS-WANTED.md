# Photos wanted (tabled players)

Players we have searched for thoroughly (Google and Bing Images, Flickr,
NBA.com and team sites, Pro-Football-Reference, news photo galleries) without
finding a usable photo. They stay in the dataset but are not drawn until they
have both photos (docs/DATA.md §Photos). The owner may send links later; any
host is fine, and we keep our own copy if it cannot be hotlinked.

What counts: a **playing photo** is him in a real NBA/NFL game for a pro team
he played for; a **headshot** is a posed portrait or a close-up of his bare
head (no helmet) in pro gear. His name may show; his college may not.
