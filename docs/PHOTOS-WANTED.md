# Photos wanted (tabled players)

Players we have searched for thoroughly without finding a usable photo. They
stay in the dataset but are not drawn until they have both photos
(docs/DATA.md §Photos). The owner may send links later; any host is fine, and
we keep our own copy if it cannot be hotlinked.

Searched so far: Google and Bing Images (top ~20 per query), Flickr, NBA.com
and team sites (current and archived on the Wayback Machine, incl. the old
`nba.com/media/act_<name>.jpg` player photos), Pro-Football-Reference and
Basketball-Reference, news photo galleries, YouTube video thumbnails and
frames, trading-card scans, and Getty Images previews (the owner allows
watermarked Getty previews as a last resort, self-hosted).

What counts: a **playing photo** is him in a real NBA/NFL game for a pro team
he played for (preseason OK; Summer League as a fallback); a **headshot** is a
posed portrait or a close-up of his bare head (no helmet) in pro gear. His
name may show; his college may not.
